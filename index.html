About us

Growing villages together

6+years of youth-led programs

Kebun Lembah Hijau is a sample farm community that empowers young people to be agents of change through sustainable agriculture, rural community development and local economic innovation.

We run programs in education, community empowerment and market access for young farmers, together with government, campuses, companies and villages.

40+partner farmers (sample)
5flagship programs

Why we exist.

Farmers are getting older and earning little. We bring the next generation back with a model that joins three strengths.

Agro

Sustainable practice, simple technology and superior local products.

Socio

Community capacity, education, local knowledge and inclusive collaboration.

Preneur

Youth creativity, local business models, capital and networks.

  • 02Farmers earn little added value from their harvest
  • 03Markets, technology and innovation are out of reach
  • 04Huge agricultural potential is still undeveloped

Flagship programs

Support a program

Five programs that work as one ecosystem, from the classroom in the field to the market shelf.

Education and agricultural training: mentoring young farmers to improve their knowledge and skills.

Community empowerment: building strong village institutions through active community participation.

Product development: turning local harvests into value-added products that compete in the market.

Market access and partnerships: connecting farmers with markets, partners and wider distribution.

Sustainable farming: environmentally friendly practices that protect natural resources.
